Pyrium: Ore + Magma Geode (Keep it hot)

Here is a "RuneScape Palette" specifically tuned for your shader setup.

Since your ambient light is Blue-ish, I have pushed the "Warm" colors (Dirt/Sand) slightly more yellow/orange to prevent them from looking purple in the shadows.

🌿 Grasses (The "R" Channel)
Classic OSRS Green: #527038 (The gold standard. Not too bright, not too dead.)

Lush / Stylized: #6EA335 (Brighter, healthier. Good for safe zones.)

Dead / Swamp: #4A4F38 (Desaturated and brownish. Good for the Wilderness.)

Autumn / Savannah: #8F8743 (Yellowish dry grass.)

🟤 Dirt & Paths (The "G" Channel)
Standard Path: #5C4633 (The classic "Road" color.)

Red Clay: #754C38 (Good for mining areas or canyons.)

Wet Mud: #36291F (Dark and rich. Use near water.)

Sand / Beach: #A89F71 (Muted yellow. Don't use neon yellow, it looks like pee.)

🌑 Stone & Base (The "Base" / Black Channel)
Standard Rock: #454545 (Perfect neutral grey background.)

Warm Grey: #524E48 (Earthy rock. Blends better with dirt.)

Cold / Dungeon: #383D45 (Blue-tinted stone. Scary vibe.)

Deep Abyss: #1A1A1A (Almost black. Good for pits.)

🏛️ Urban / Hard Surface (The "B" Channel)
Castle Stone: #75787B (Clean, light grey for walls/floors.)

Gravel: #6B685F (Noisy, brownish grey.)

Old Concrete: #767873 (Green-tinted grey, looks mossy/old.)

Pavement: #4A4A52 (Dark blue-grey, good for "modern" or magical ruins.)

🎨 My Recommended "Starter Palette"
Configure your shader with these 4 to start your Basin area:

Color Base (Black): #454545 (Standard Rock) -> Cliffs & Mountains

Color R (Red Paint): #527038 (Classic Green) -> Grass

Color G (Green Paint): #5C4633 (Standard Path) -> Roads & Dirt patches

Color B (Blue Paint): #6B685F (Gravel) -> River beds or Cave floors
